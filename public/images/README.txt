A Little Universe Called Sowmi - Photo Directory
========================================================================

Place your photographs directly inside this folder (`public/images/`):

1. Solo Photographs of Sowmiya:
   - `sowmi-01.jpg` : First solo photo of Sowmi
   - `sowmi-02.jpg` : Second solo photo of Sowmi
   - `sowmi-03.jpg` : Third solo photo of Sowmi

2. Wedding Photograph (Together):
   - `our-memory.jpg` : The photograph of Sowmi and you at the wedding

3. Optional additional photos:
   You can add more photos simply by editing `src/data/galleryData.js`!
   
Note:
If an image file is not present yet, the website will automatically
display a beautiful, celestial-themed placeholder card with instructions,
ensuring zero broken image icons!
